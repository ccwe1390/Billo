## 2. Architecture & policy findings (outrank section 7)

### A1. The number lock checks membership, not position (audit-2 #12, still open)
numlock.py:34-48 compares SETS of numbers. A translation that swaps income and guideline,
adds "NOT", or splits "44" with a zero-width space passes (D and C reproduced:
check(...) -> (True, [], [])). Patching one trick at a time is a treadmill; an ordered
per-line comparison is a design change. The pitch says "every number is checked". Question:
change the pitch wording now ("every number must be one the rules engine produced") and
treat the ordered check as F11 work, or build the ordered check before 1:00 PM?

### A2. "Nothing is stored on our server" is broader than the system
- The app code keeps no bodies (C verified by grep two ways).
- But Apache on the VM writes the standard "combined" access log: client IP, time, URL and
  user-agent. S checked over SSH: /var/log/httpd/access_log has 628 lines, with logrotate
  defaults.
- A chunked upload with no Content-Length is also spooled to a temp file before the 413
  (C reproduced: rollovers 1).
- Bill values and answers are never logged, because they travel in POST bodies.
- Question: narrow the claim ("we keep none of your bill or answers; the web server keeps a
  standard access log"), or change the server (drop %h from LogFormat, or anonymize it)?

### A3. One presenter sheet or a banner stack?
docs/2026-10-17_pitch-corrections.md now has 4 dated banners over a body whose rows
contradict them (QR, Home and dashboard "not built", "11 languages", "2 bills").
HANDOFF.md:109 says do NOT hand it to presenters; HANDOFF.md:121-122 says give it to them.
Each new fact adds a banner, and the body stays wrong. Question: rewrite the body once
into a live-true sheet (M effort), or keep banners and accept that presenters must read
top-down?
