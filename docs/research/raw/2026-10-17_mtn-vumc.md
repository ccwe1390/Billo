# Vanderbilt (VUMC) system: Middle Tennessee hospitals, financial assistance policy (FAP) study

Header: researched and accessed 2026-10-17 (CDT). Question: what are the current FAP terms at each VUMC-owned hospital in Middle Tennessee? Audience: the orchestrator compiling the 5-agent study, which feeds Bill to Help (TNHacks27). Scratch copies of every PDF and its text are in `<session scratchpad>\vf\`. The file `financial-assistance-policy-english.txt` there is the one to grep.

## TL;DR
- VUMC runs one system-wide FAP covering 7 hospitals: Vanderbilt University Hospital (VUH), Monroe Carell Jr. Children's Hospital (Carell), Vanderbilt Psychiatric Hospital (VPH), Wilson County (VWCH), Bedford (VBCH/VBH), Tullahoma-Harton (VTHH) and Clarksville (VCH).
- The rival hypothesis, a separate policy per hospital, died. Only the AGB percentage is per-hospital, and VCH gets its own appendix.
- Thresholds are 100% discount at or below 300% of the Federal Poverty Guideline (FPG) and 85% discount from 300% to 350%, with nothing above 350% except catastrophic assistance. VCH gives 100% up to 350%.
- The 400% FPL and 200% FPL figures on third-party sites do not match this document. I did not retrieve the older policy to confirm, so treat those as stale or unverified.
- The policy contradicts itself on the application window: the definition says 240 days, the operative text says 120 days. It has no residency test and no asset test (searched in English and Spanish).
- Every number here is the hospital's own published policy (VENDOR-SELF-REPORTED, the strongest evidence for what the policy says). No independent data on actual approval practice was found.
- Williamson Medical Center is not VUMC-owned.
