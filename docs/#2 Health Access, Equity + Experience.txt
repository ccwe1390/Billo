How might we use technology to remove friction from the health journey so that individuals and communities can more easily access,
understand, coordinate, and act on the support they need for better health and well-being?

Why this matters
Health outcomes are shaped by far more than a clinical encounter. People
may encounter barriers in finding care, understanding information,
navigating services, coordinating support, accessing transportation,
communicating across languages, or maintaining trust in the systems
designed to help them.


Who could benefit
Individuals, patients, families, and caregivers

Clinicians, care teams, and healthcare administrators

Community-based organizations and public-health partners

Healthcare, insurance, and life-sciences organizations


Challenge

Build a scalable solution that improves health access, equity, experience,
coordination, prevention, or operational effectiveness. Teams may focus on
a specific point of friction - such as health literacy, access to services, care
coordination, community health, accessibility, workforce capacity,
preventive care, or social determinants of health. Teams should go deep on
a clearly defined problem, user group, and measurable outcome.

OCI / Oracle opportunity
Use OCI Generative AI and privacy technologies to synthesize information, surface
insights, personalize experiences, or streamline workflows in ways that respect the
sensitivity of health-related information. Teams may also use OCI to build and scale
a thoughtful, accessible experience that connects people, care teams, and
community resources.


Responsible innovation
Use synthetic, public, or appropriately de-identified data. Solutions should not
make autonomous diagnostic or treatment decisions. Consider privacy,
transparency, accessibility, bias mitigation, and appropriate human oversight from
the outset.


What success looks like
A compelling health or well-being problem with clear social value

A usable prototype with a credible path to scale

Improved access, understanding, coordination, or experience

Thoughtful, responsible use of AI

Meaningful application of Oracle and OCI capabilities
