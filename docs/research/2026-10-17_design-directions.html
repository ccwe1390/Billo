Bill to Help: three design directions for the Result screen

SYNTHETIC DEMO. Every mock below uses a fictional hospital (Cumberland General, synthetic) and made-up numbers: household of 4, $38,000 a year, 115% of the 2026 federal poverty guideline.

Fonts: this page makes no network requests, so all text uses system fonts as stand-ins (system-ui / Segoe UI / Georgia). The intended self-hosted fonts are named in each spec box. Icons are omitted on purpose; each spec names the open-licensed set it would use.

Arabic and Chinese text is a draft written for this preview and needs review by a fluent speaker before any use (the Chinese reuses the app's own zh-CN strings where they exist). Policy text stays in English (as the app does today), isolated inside the translated layout.

Direction 1 (recommended) also has a Simplified Chinese (zh-CN) mock. Its CJK fonts are the real production choice, not a stand-in: the brief recommends the phone's own Chinese font over a CJK webfont.

Companion brief: docs/research/2026-10-17_design-system-brief.md (sources, contrast ratios, build cost, recommendation).

1. Paper Trail

The result reads like a well-made document: the hospital's own policy table, with your row highlighted and every claim footnoted to its page.

Signals trust to
The physician and Vanderbilt faculty (citations, sources first); families who already trust paper.

2. Public Counter

A service-desk system in the GOV.UK / USWDS tradition: one decision per screen, the answer first in huge type, and a sliding-scale bar that shows where you fall.

Signals trust to
The HCA application-services director and Oracle executives (rigor, workflow clarity, accessibility); public-service users.
Colors (ratios in the brief)
  • ink #1B1B1B
  • river #00597A
  • go #1B6B3A
  • focus gold #FFBE2E
  • ink-2 #565C65
  • line #71767A
Intended type
Noto Sans (Latin, Vietnamese, Cyrillic, Devanagari) + Noto Sans Arabic, self-hosted; system CJK fonts as in Direction 1; 800-weight headings.
Signature
Sliding-scale bar built from the policy's real bands; segmented step bar; a heavy "answer block" with a thick inline-start rule; no cards anywhere.

3. Front Porch

A warm community-clinic voice: the result is a sentence spoken to the helper, followed by a short numbered to-do list with the deadline as the one bright item.

Signals trust to
Families and the adult child helping a parent (warmth, plain speech); weaker with the executives and the physician.
Colors (ratios in the brief)
  • ink #10302A
  • green #0F6B5C
  • marigold #F2B035
  • surface #F1F6F4
  • ink-2 #4D615C
  • edge #5F7A73
Intended type
Readex Pro (Latin, Vietnamese, Arabic) + Noto Sans for Cyrillic and Devanagari; system CJK fonts as in Direction 1.
Signature
Marigold underline under the key numbers; numbered next-steps list where only the deadline step is filled; pill language toggle.
